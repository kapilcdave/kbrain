# Capacity Versus Edge

## Summary

The largest quoted edge was rarely available at meaningful size. Attractive
returns tended to occur in thin contracts, while deep markets offered smaller
premia. Capacity must be measured independently from per-unit edge.

## Findings

- **Measured:** Candidate rankings based on median spread multiplied by total
  volume materially overstated capacity.
- **Measured:** In multiple studies, the largest per-contract effects occurred
  in the lowest-volume groups; volume weighting weakened or removed the result.
- **Measured:** Crypto carry with persistent rates was capped by the thinner
  hedge venue, while deep major assets offered returns near cash alternatives.
- **Inference:** Edge and attainable size are often negatively related.
- **Conclusion:** Report realizable depth or volume-weighted return, never mean
  edge multiplied by aggregate volume.

## Limits

Displayed depth can be cancelled, candle volume does not identify queue
ownership, and historical flow is only an upper bound on fills.
