# About

I am Kapil Dave, a software engineer who likes turning broad ideas into systems
that can be measured and falsified.

My recent work sits at the intersection of AI agents, market infrastructure,
quantitative research, and small useful products. I build the collector, the
model, the execution simulator, the operational controls, and the postmortem.
The recurring goal is not to produce the most exciting result. It is to find
out what remains true after realistic constraints are added.

That approach has produced:

- agent-memory and coordination infrastructure;
- terminal and web products;
- privacy-conscious telemetry tooling;
- research systems spanning weather, sports, crypto, and options;
- a large catalogue of negative results and corrected conclusions;
- production patterns built around explicit arming, reconciliation, and caps.

The negative results are central to the work. They document where fees absorb
predictability, where apparent edge is a timestamp bug, where queue position
changes the sign, and where positive unit economics have no meaningful size.
