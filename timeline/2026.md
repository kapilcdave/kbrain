# 2026 Timeline

## Broad Market Research

Research expanded across weather, sports, short-duration prediction markets,
cross-venue relationships, options, and crypto. Most forecasting models matched
or underperformed market prices after executable costs.

Several strong-looking results were traced to timing leakage, settlement values
used as prices, loose joins, non-causal features, and optimistic fill models.
Those failures motivated stricter data and execution standards.

## Holdouts and the Research Graveyard

Chronological holdouts and formal preregistrations became standard. Viewed
windows were marked as burned, and failures were classified by cause instead of
discarded. A consolidated handoff documented closed strategies, structural
barriers, retractions, and reusable methodology.

## Passive Execution and Perpetuals

Passive candidates were tested against realistic queue and markout assumptions.
Apparent spread capture repeatedly failed after adverse selection. A separate
perpetual-maker study was evaluated once under frozen rules; all assets failed,
so its planned canary was never activated.

## Capacity Becomes the Constraint

Some narrow effects survived historical tests, but throughput, concentration,
or available size bound their usefulness. The research began reporting
volume-weighted effects and realizable depth rather than multiplying mean edge
by total market volume.

## Crypto Market Structure

DEX/CEX arbitrage, redemption, funding carry, liquidity provision, compounding
products, and options were evaluated as distinct families. Most arbitrage was
closed by fees, existing competition, redemption mechanics, hedging costs, or
capacity. Surviving returns were better described as risk premia.

## Agent and Compute Infrastructure

Agent Memory Coordination tested shared semantic intent for parallel agents.
Hedge Router turned privacy-filtered gateway telemetry into a paper-first
compute-exposure research pipeline.

## Public Synthesis

`kbrain` began as a curated public layer over the durable work: project context,
methodology, negative results, and corrections without raw private memory or
operational state.
