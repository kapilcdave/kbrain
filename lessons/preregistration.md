# Preregistration

Preregistration separates hypothesis generation from evaluation. It does not
guarantee a correct result, but it makes later changes visible.

## Freeze Before Testing

- Hypothesis and motivating evidence
- Prior searches and selection liabilities
- Market universe and evaluation dates
- Causal feature, entry, exit, and holding rules
- Executable-price and cost conventions
- Independent unit and clustering level
- Primary endpoint and controls
- Sample, breadth, and concentration requirements
- Pass, fail, underpowered, and blocked responses

## Holdouts Are Consumable

Once viewed, a holdout is burned. A change to the assets, dates, thresholds,
side, or filters creates a new hypothesis requiring a new future window.
Renaming a post-hoc cut does not restore independence.

Collectors and scorers can be tested beforehand on synthetic data or disclosed
partial dry runs for schema and unit errors. Those checks cannot select
parameters or preview the authoritative outcome.

The value is not paperwork. It creates a verifiable boundary between deciding
what to test and learning what happened.
