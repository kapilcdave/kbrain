# Negative Results

Most ideas did not become deployable strategies. Preserving why they failed
prevents repeated work and makes surviving evidence more credible.

## Failure Classes

- **No edge:** the relationship does not survive controls.
- **Overfit:** discovery disappears on unseen time.
- **Cost barrier:** a real signal is smaller than the trading toll.
- **Adverse selection:** passive fills lose more than they capture.
- **Capacity:** positive unit economics exist only without usable size.
- **Contract mismatch:** compared instruments have different payoffs.
- **Shared market:** supposed venues use the same underlying book.
- **Data defect:** leakage, timing, schema, or accounting created the result.
- **Underpowered:** independent evidence cannot resolve the claim.
- **Blocked:** a decisive term cannot be measured.
- **Safety constraint:** the test cannot be performed responsibly.

These outcomes permit different next steps. Underpowered allows unchanged data
collection. No edge does not. A cost barrier can remain a useful market-structure
finding even when no trade survives.

The goal is not to maximize positive claims. It is to reduce uncertainty without
promoting conclusions the evidence cannot support.
